This app uses openly licensed data made by other people. This file credits
each source and states its license. Each license's terms apply to that data,
not to the app's own code.

## Example sentences and audio

**hsk-sentences-audio**, by the hsk-sentences-audio contributors
https://github.com/no7z/hsk-sentences-audio

- **Sentences, pinyin, translations, glosses, and grammar tags**: licensed under
  [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
- **Audio**: generated with the CosyVoice2-0.5B text-to-speech model
  (voice `zh-female-studio`), recorded in the dataset as Apache-2.0.
- **Changes made:** sentences were re-segmented, re-tagged, and re-leveled
  against the HSK 2021 word list. They were grouped into units, linked to
  dictionary senses, and their pinyin was converted to tone-sandhi form.
  Each sentence keeps its original dataset ID (`external_id`).

Because the sentence data is CC BY-SA, any modified copy of it that we
redistribute (for example, a published export of the database) must also be
released under CC BY-SA 4.0 with this credit.

## AI-assisted content

Some word senses were matched to sentences with an AI model (Claude Haiku).
Definitions the AI wrote itself, where no dictionary meaning fit, are marked
`ai_added` in the database.
