The warm-up exercise asks students to match pictures (labeled A-F) with words. The pictures are described as: A=Chinese characters/text, B=food dishes, C=teacher greeting students, D=woman writing on a board, E=woman cooking, F=mother with baby. The words are: 1=妈妈, 2=汉字, 3=中国菜, 4=说汉语, 5=写汉字, 6=做中国菜.

Matching: 1妈妈→F, 2汉字→A, 3中国菜→B, 4说汉语→C, 5写汉字→D, 6做中国菜→E

For the picture description exercise (Exercise 3), the blanks require personal/open-ended answers based on actual situations, except the last one which has two blanks. Looking at the text dialogues for clues:
- "我们都会___。" — open-ended (what they can do)
- "中国菜很___。" — from text: "中国菜很好吃" → 好吃
- "他会做___。" — open-ended (what he can cook)
- "这个汉字我会___，不会___。" — from text: "这个字我会读，不会写" → 读, 写
