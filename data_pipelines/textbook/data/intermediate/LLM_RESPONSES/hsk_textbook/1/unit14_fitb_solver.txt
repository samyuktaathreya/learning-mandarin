The warm-up sentences (1–6) ask students to match pictures to words by writing a letter (A–F) in the blank. The correct letters can be determined from the illustration descriptions: apples→D, car→A, clothes→B, store→F, bookstore→E, English book→C.

However, the blanks are meant to hold picture labels (A–F), which are index labels, not words. These are matching exercises where the answer is a letter label, not a word from a word bank mapping to a Chinese word. Since the answers are picture labels (not resolvable to Chinese words), and the exercise is a matching task, I'll drop those six.

For the remaining four:
- "妈妈 去 商店 买了___。" — open-ended, student fills in what mom bought; no single correct answer derivable from text. Drop.
- "她 下午 去 学___了。" — the picture shows a woman driving, and Text 2 mentions "去学开车了", so the answer is "开车".
- "桌子 上___是 张 先生 的 东西。" — from Note 4 example (3): "这些都是王方的东西" uses "都"; the blank needs an adverb. The sentence structure matches "这些都是..." but here subject is "桌子上", and the blank is between 上 and 是. Looking at the note on "都": "所总括的对象必须放在'都'的前面". The blank fits "都".
- "丽丽买了___衣服。" — open-ended (how much clothing), no single answer. But Text 3 has "她买了不少衣服" and the picture shows a woman with shopping bags similar to Wang Fang's description. The exercise says to describe pictures using newly-learned language points — "不少" is explicitly taught. This is answerable as "不少".
