The warm-up sentences (1–6) ask students to match pictures to words by writing a letter (A–F) in the blank. The correct matches can be determined from the unit text:

- 苹果 (apple) → D (apples photo)
- 车 (car) → A (cars parked photo)
- 衣服 (clothes) → B (clothes on hangers photo)
- 商店 (store) → F (shopping mall interior photo)
- 书店 (bookstore) → E (bookstore interior photo)
- 英语书 (English book) → C (books photo)

However, these are picture-matching exercises where the answer is a letter label (A–F) referring to a photo, not a word from a word bank. The labels A–F here refer to pictures, not words, so the answers would be picture labels — not resolvable to Chinese words. These must be dropped per the rules.

For the description sentences (Exercise 3), the blanks require personal/open-ended answers or context not fully determinable from the text alone, except where the dialogues provide clear answers.

- "桌子 上___是 张 先生 的 东西。" — The adverb "都" fits here, supported by Note 4 example (3): "这些都是王方的东西" and the dialogue context.
- "丽丽买了___衣服。" — "不少" fits, supported by the text "她买了不少衣服."
- "妈妈 去 商店 买了___。" and "她 下午 去 学___了。" — open-ended, no single correct answer derivable from unit text.
