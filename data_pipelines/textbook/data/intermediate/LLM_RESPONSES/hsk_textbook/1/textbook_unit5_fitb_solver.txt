The warm-up matching exercise (candidates 1-6) asks students to match words to pictures labeled A-F, but the unit text does not specify which picture label corresponds to which word — the answer depends on viewing the actual illustrations, which are not described in the OCR text. So those must be dropped.

The remaining candidates (7-12) are open-ended description exercises where students fill in based on photos they are viewing, with no single correct answer derivable from the unit text alone. They must also be dropped.
