The warm-up exercise asks students to match pictures A-F with the words. The pictures are described as "a cat, dog, chair, hospital room, doctor, and father with child" in order A-F. So:
- 爸爸 (father with child) = F
- 医生 (doctor) = E
- 医院 (hospital room) = D
- 椅子 (chair) = C
- 猫 (cat) = A
- 狗 (dog) = B

For the "describe the pictures" exercise, the blanks are open-ended (students fill in based on what they see in illustrations), and the answers depend on the specific pictures which can't be fully determined from text alone. The last two also require personal/open-ended responses.
