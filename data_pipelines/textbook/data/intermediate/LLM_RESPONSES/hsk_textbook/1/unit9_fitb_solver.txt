The warm-up candidates (爸爸, 医生, etc.) ask students to match pictures to words, but the correct picture letter (A–F) for each word is not stated in the unit text — only the pictures are described generically. These cannot be answered with high confidence from the text alone.

The exercise 3 candidates (___在___。 etc.) are open-ended description exercises where students fill in based on pictures; the unit text does not provide the specific answers for those blanks.
