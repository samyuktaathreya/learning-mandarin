## 2-07
**Point:** Demonstrative adverbs 这样/那样/这么/那么 expressing manner, degree, or hypothetical condition
**Covers:** 
- 这样/那样 + verb (manner: "this/that way")
- 这么/那么 + adjective (degree emphasis: "so/that")
- 这样 in consequential clauses with 会/才 ("this way will/only")
- 这样的话 for hypothetical "if this way"

**Does not cover:** 那么 used as a discourse connector meaning "then/so" in response to a preceding clause (那么 + suggestion/decision), which is a different grammar point about logical sequence rather than manner/degree/hypothesis

```rule
match: (这样|那样|这么|那么)/AD ... (VA|VV)
match: (这样|这么)/AD 的话
match: (这样|那样)/AD VV
unless: ^ (那么)/AD (我们|我|他|你|大家|他们) (VV|VC)
unless: ^ (那么)/AD (这|是)
unless: ^ (那么)/AD (就|让|开始|通过)
```

**Notes:** 
- The first match pattern captures the core uses: demonstrative adverb followed eventually by a verb or adjective in the same clause
- The second match covers 这样/这么 + 的话 pattern for hypothetical use
- The third match is redundant with the first but included for clarity on the canonical "这样/那样 + VV" pattern
- The unless clauses specifically block the "那么 as discourse connector" pattern: 那么 appearing after clause boundaries followed by pronouns + decisions/suggestions (VV for actions, VC for evaluations like 是)
- The pattern is deliberately broad to avoid false negatives, relying on the unless clauses for precision
- Some sentences may tag 这样 as PN or VV instead of AD (tagging errors); the rule accepts this risk to avoid missing valid examples, since "那么/这么 acting as discourse connectors" in the unless clauses are the key pattern to block
