## 1-21
**Point:** Aspect particle 了 marking completion of an action (dynamic aspect)
**Covers:** Sentences with 了 immediately after a verb (with or without an object), or after an object, expressing that an action has been completed. Includes: simple completion (Verb + 了 + Obj), completion with time/duration (Verb + 了 + Duration + Obj), completion with quantified objects (Verb + 了 + Num + Measure + Obj), and yes/no questions with 了吗.
**Does not cover:** Change of state (adjective + 了), expressing "now" (知道了), expressing "not anymore" (不/没 + Verb + 了), potential complements (得了/不了), and other uses of 了 that are not marking action completion.

**Notes:** This rule detects completion 了 by looking for patterns where 了 (tagged AS) appears after a verb (VV), typically followed by an object or object-related elements (nouns, numbers with measure words, time expressions, quantifiers, determiners). The unless clauses exclude state change (adjectives + 了), potential complements (得了/不了), negation patterns (不/没 + Verb + 了), and imperative structures (别 + Verb + 了). The rule avoids false matches on sentence-final 了 (SP tag) in state change contexts while still capturing completion 了 when it appears as SP at sentence end with an object earlier in the clause.
