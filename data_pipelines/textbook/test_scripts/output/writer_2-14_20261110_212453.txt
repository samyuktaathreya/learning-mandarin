## 2-14
**Point:** Range and cooperative adverbs (全, 一共, 只) indicating entirety, totality, or exclusivity
**Covers:** 
- 全/全都 indicating "the whole/entire" of something (with or without 都)
- 只/只有 meaning "only" (as basic adverb before verbs, or with 有 meaning "only have")
- 一共 meaning "altogether/in total"

**Does not cover:** 
- 只好 (forced to, had no choice but)
- 只要 (as long as, if only)
- 只有...才 (only if...then) as a conditional structure
- 不只/不但...而且 (not only...but also)
- 只 as a measure word or as part of other phrases (这只是, 一只虫子)
- 全 in compound words (全力, 齐全, 完全) or as 全部/全国/全校used attributively without 都
- 一共 when used as predicate meaning "altogether" without showing totality of a set

```rule
match: 全/DT ... 都/AD
match: 全都/AD
match: 只/AD _ /VV|VA
match: 只/AD 会/VV|能/VV
match: 只有/AD _ /CD
match: 一共/AD
unless: 只好/AD
unless: 只要/CS
unless: 只有/CS
unless: 不/AD 只/VV
unless: 不/AD 只/AD 是/VC
```

**Notes:** 
- The rule prioritizes precision. It matches 全都 as a single token and 全+DT+都 patterns (the main structures for "whole/entire").
- For 只: matches basic adverb-before-verb patterns (只 + verb, 只 + 会/能 + verb) and 只有 + number + measure + noun (meaning "only have"). This avoids 只好, 只要, and 只有...才 structures which are separate grammar points.
- 只有/AD indicates 只有 as adverb (only have); 只有/CS tags it as subordinating conjunction (only if...then).
- The unless clauses exclude the confounding grammar points: 只好, 只要 (both can be tagged as CS or AD), and 只有 when functioning as a conditional connector.
- Counter-example "只是" is avoided because 只 + 是 is a copula phrase, not the adverb use.
- One-word 全都 and patterns like 没全都 are caught by match: 全都/AD.
