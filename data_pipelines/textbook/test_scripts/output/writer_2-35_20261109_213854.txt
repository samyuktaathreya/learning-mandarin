## 2-35
**Point:** Using 的话 (and variants with 如果/要是/假如) to express conditional "if" statements, or using 等 to indicate "etc."

**Covers:** 
- Conditional clauses with 的话 (with or without 如果/要是/假如/etc.), optionally followed by 就 in the consequent clause
- Sentences using 等 as "et cetera" marker after a list of items

**Does not cover:** 
- Topic marking with 的话 (e.g., "北京的话，秋天去最好" where 的话 marks a topic for comment, not a condition)
- 的话 when it means "words/speech" as a noun (tagged DEG, not SP)
- Other conditional structures not using 的话 or 等

```rule
match: 的话/SP
match: 等/ETC
unless: ^ ... 的话/SP ‖ ... /VC|VV|VA|VE ...
unless: /PN|NN|NR|NT 的话/SP ‖ /AD|VA
```

**Notes:** The first `unless` line excludes the topic-marking pattern by checking that a clause starting with a noun/pronoun followed by 的话 is followed by a comment clause (containing only descriptive predicates, not action verbs or copulas that would indicate a conditional consequent). The second `unless` catches cases where a single nominal or temporal expression + 的话 appears at clause start as a topic marker. The rule prioritizes precision: it will match clear conditional uses with 的话 (especially those with 如果 or 就, or with predicate verbs before 的话), and any use of 等, while excluding the most common topic-marking false positives.
