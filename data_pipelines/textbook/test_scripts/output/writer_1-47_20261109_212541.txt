## 1-47
**Point:** Using 还是 to offer choices in questions
**Covers:** Questions presenting two or more options where the asker expects the respondent to choose one, including yes/no questions with 是...还是 structure and questions with implied verbs/actions
**Does not cover:** Uses of 还是 meaning "had better" (with 吧), "still/yet" in continuation, "after all" in statements, or other adverbial uses where it doesn't present alternatives to choose between

```rule
match: 还是/CC
unless: /SP$ ... 还是/CC
unless: CS ... 还是
unless: ... 还是/CC ... 吧/SP
unless: ... 又/CC ... 还是/AD
unless: 还是/AD
unless: ... 了/AS 又 ... 还是
```

**Notes:** 
- The key distinguishing feature is 还是/CC (tagged as coordinating conjunction) vs 还是/AD (tagged as adverb). This point requires CC.
- Counter-examples show 还是/AD in various contexts: after 尽管 concessive clauses, before 吧 for "had better", after 又 in repeated action patterns, and in general continuative statements (尽管...还是, 但是...还是, etc.).
- Some sentences may have multiple clauses; the rule checks within a single clause.
- The unless clauses catch: sentence-final 吧 patterns, concessive contexts with CS, repeated action patterns with 又, and the adverbial uses tagged AD.
- Edge case: "他闻了又闻，还是什么都没闻到" has 还是/CC but is likely a counter-example due to the 又 pattern in previous clause — the second unless clause handles this.
- The rule is deliberately strict to avoid false positives, which is worse for teaching than missing borderline cases.
