## 2-31
**Point:** Degree complement (得) to modify how a verb is done or to what degree an adjective applies
**Covers:** Verb + 得 + degree modifier (adverb + adjective, or adjective alone); Adjective + 得 + intensifier (极了, 死了, etc.); structures with repeated verbs when object is present
**Does not cover:** Modal 得 (have to); 值得 (worth); 不得不 (must); 显得 (appear); potential complements (得/不 + directional/ability verb); complement 着; 得 as auxiliary verb in other constructions; comparison intensifier (得多)

```rule
match: /VV /DER /VA
match: /VA /DER /AD|VA
unless: 值得/VV
unless: 不得不/AD
unless: 显得/VV
unless: /VV /DER /VV
unless: /DER 着/VV
unless: /DER 多/VA
unless: /VV /DER /VV /SP
```

**Notes:** 
- The first pattern captures the most common HSK 2 use: verb + 得 + degree word (e.g., 跑得快, 吃得太饱, 说得很好)
- The second pattern captures adjectives taking degree complements (e.g., 可爱死了, 高兴得很, 舒服极了)
- Unless lines exclude other 得 uses: 值得/显得 (different grammar points), 不得不 (modal), potential complements with directional/ability verbs (得来, 得着, 得下, 得上), and comparison 得多
- The pattern /VV /DER /VV is excluded because it catches potential complements (看得见, 做得好吗as potential) and other non-degree uses
- Some borderline cases like "看得见" (potential complement) should not match due to the /VV /DER /VV exclusion
- Sentences with verb repetition (你写字写得很漂亮) will match because the second verb instance + 得 + adjective pattern matches
