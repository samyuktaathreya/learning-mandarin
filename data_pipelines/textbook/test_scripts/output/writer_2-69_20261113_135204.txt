## 2-69
**Point:** Events in quick succession with "yi... jiu..." (一⋯⋯就⋯⋯)
**Covers:** Sentences where 一 marks the onset of the first event and 就 marks the immediate consequence, showing that as soon as Event 1 happens, Event 2 follows in quick succession. The structure shows causation or temporal sequence between two events.

**Notes:** The core pattern requires 一 (tagged AD, not CD) immediately before or within the first clause's verb phrase, followed by 就 (tagged AD) in a subsequent clause or later in the same clause. The 一 is an adverbial marker of "every time/as soon as," not a number (CD). The pattern must capture both clauses separated by ‖ (punctuation break) and cases within a single clause. Multiple unless clauses exclude similar-looking but different grammar points. The optional clause break (‖?) accommodates both linked and non-linked structures. The pattern `一/AD _ /VV` allows for intervening words like objects between 一 and the verb.
