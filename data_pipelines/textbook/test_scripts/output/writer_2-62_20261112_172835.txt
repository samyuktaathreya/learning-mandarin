## 2-62
**Point:** Sequencing consecutive events or actions (承接复句)
**Covers:** Sentences showing two or more sequential actions/events connected by conjunctions like 先...再, 然后, 接着, or by simple juxtaposition with punctuation. Includes patterns with 以后 showing sequence after a completed event.
**Does not cover:** Single actions with 再 meaning "again" or "once more"; 再说 meaning "in addition"; 一再/再三 meaning "repeatedly"; 再也不/没 meaning "never again"; 再不...就 meaning conditional threat; other uses of 再 as a future-tense marker.

The rule prioritizes precision by excluding patterns likely to be other grammar points.
