## 1-04
**Point:** Interrogative pronouns used to ask questions: 怎么 / 怎么样, 几, 多少, 谁, 什么, 哪 / 哪儿 / 哪里 / 哪些. The point also covers 几 meaning "several" and the money forms 多少钱 and 块…毛.

**Covers:**
- 怎么 before a verb or adjective, meaning "how" or "why", including 怎么这么/那么 + adj.
- 怎么样 as a predicate.
- 几 + measure word or number (几个, 几十, 几百家, 好几次, 几点).
- 多少 as a real question.
- 是谁 and a clause-initial 谁.
- A clause-final 什么 after a verb or 是.
- 哪 + measure word (哪个, 哪位).
- Clause-final 哪里 / 哪儿.
- Single-word 怎么 / 为什么 / 什么.
- Money amounts of the form 块 + number + 毛.

**Does not cover:**
- 不怎么 / 没怎么, which is a different point.
- 怎么 / 多少 / 谁 / 哪 followed by 都 or 也, which is the "every / any" use.
- 什么的 and X什么X challenges.
- Indefinite "some" uses such as 点什么 or 在哪儿见过.
- 没什么, which means "nothing".
- 好/VA + 几, because the tagger mis-tags it in sentences from other points.

```rule
match: 怎么/AD
match: 怎么样/VV
match: 几/CD M
match: 几/CD CD
match: 好/AD 几/CD
match: 多少/CD
match: 是/VC 谁/PN
match: ^ 谁/PN
match: 哪/DT M
match: 哪些
match: 哪里|哪儿/PN $
match: /VV 什么/PN $
match: 是/VC 什么/PN $
match: 块|元/M CD 毛|分|角/M
match: ^ 怎么|为什么|什么 $
unless: 不 怎么
unless: 没 怎么
unless: 不 怎么样
unless: 怎么 ... 都|也/AD
unless: 多少 ... 都|也/AD
unless: 谁 ... 都|也/AD
unless: 哪 ... 都|也/AD
unless: 哪儿|哪里 ... 都|也/AD
unless: 什么 的
unless: 什么 ... 都|也/AD
unless: 点|点儿|些 ... 什么
unless: 没 什么
unless: 好/VA 几
unless: 什么 _ 什么
```

**Notes:**
- Precision is favoured, so common patterns are left out: 什么 + noun, 什么时候, 哪里/哪儿 before a verb, and 谁 after a verb other than 是. They are too easily the indefinite "some" use.
- The whole-sentence `unless` lines are clause-limited, so 都 in a different clause does not block a match.
- 好/VA 几 is excluded because it appears in a counter-example sentence; 好/AD
