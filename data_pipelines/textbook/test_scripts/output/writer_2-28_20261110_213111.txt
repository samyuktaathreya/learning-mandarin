## 2-28
<!-- written by claude-sonnet-5-5 on 2026-11-09; edit freely -->
**Point:** The preposition 为 (wèi), "for", introduces the person or party an action is done for: 为 + party + Verb/Adj. This rule covers 为 only. The 给 + recipient + verb pattern belongs to easier points, so it is not matched here.

**Covers:** 为 tagged as a preposition before a person or thing and then a verb or adjective. Examples: 为你高兴, 为公司提供保障, 是为你准备的, 为美国政府工作, 为大家干杯.

**Does not cover:**
- 给 in any use (给我打电话, 给客人倒茶, 是给客人住的). These are easier points (1-xx and 2-xx), and the counter-examples show they must not match.
- 为了 (purpose), which the tagger treats as a separate token.
- 因为 (because), also a separate token.
- 作为.
- 以…为… ("take X as Y"), where 为 is tagged VV.
- 成为, 分为, 称为, 以为, 为什么, 为何, 为此, 为期 and 为难, which are all single tokens or tagged differently.
- 为 tagged VC or VV, as in 评选为模范.

```rule
match: 为/P
unless: 以 ... 为/P
```

**Notes:**
- The rule relies on the tagger giving bare 为 the tag P only in the "for" sense. The labeled counter-examples show 以…为 getting VV, but the extra `unless` line guards against a mis-tag.
- The earlier 给/P line is removed. It matched many sentences from easier grammar points (e.g. 给你打电话, 给客人倒茶), and the labeled positives did not need it.
- Bare 为 tagged VV or VC (e.g. 为荣, 评选为) is deliberately not matched.
