## 3-01
**Point:** Ordinal numbers with 第 (dì)
**Covers:** Sentences using 第 + number (+ measure word) (+ noun) to express ordinal positions like "first," "second," "third," etc.
**Does not cover:** 老 or 小 used in other contexts (频率副词 "always," age/size comparison, or as part of common nouns like 老师, 小麦, 小说); 第 used as an OD tag in other contexts

```rule
match: 第/CD ... /M
match: 第/CD ... /NN
match: 第/CD
unless: 老是/AD
unless: 老/AD
unless: 小/VA
unless: /VA 几/CD 岁/M
```

**Notes:** The rule prioritizes precision by matching the core structure: 第 followed by a CD (number) tag, optionally followed by a measure word or noun. The main match pattern captures "第 + number + measure/noun" which is the canonical form shown in examples. The standalone "第/CD" match catches cases like "第一天" and "第二行" where the ordinal directly precedes a time noun or verb. The unless clauses exclude: (1) 老是 as an adverbial marker for habitual/repetitive actions; (2) bare 老 as an adverb (频率副词); (3) 小 as a VA (adjective predicate) including age/size comparisons with 岁. This approach avoids false positives on counter-examples while capturing the intended ordinal grammar point.
