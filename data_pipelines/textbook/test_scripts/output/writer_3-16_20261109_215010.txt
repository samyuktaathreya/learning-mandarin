## 3-16
**Point:** Using 再 (zài) to express "again" for future actions, or "and then" for sequencing events
**Covers:** Future repetition ("again"), sequential ordering with 先...再..., and "another" with quantity phrases
**Does not cover:** 一再/再三 (repeatedly), 再不...就... (last chance warning), 再也不/没 (never again), 再也...也... (even so), 再说 as "in addition" (standalone at clause start)

```rule
match: 再/AD ... /VV
match: 先/AD ... 再/AD
unless: 再/AD 也/AD
unless: 再/AD 不/AD
unless: 一再/AD
unless: 再三/AD
unless: 再/AD 说/VV $
```

**Notes:** The rule matches 再 as an adverb followed by a verb in the same clause, which captures the main uses: future "again" and sequencing. The first pattern allows intervening words (objects, measure words, etc.). The second pattern specifically catches 先...再... sequencing. Exclusions filter out: 再也 combinations (never again), 再不 combinations (last chance warnings), 一再/再三 (frequency adverbs), and sentence-final 再说 (which is covered under a different HSK point about "in addition"). Counter-examples like 此后我们再没见过 (past tense with 没) should not match because the tagger likely handles 再没 as a unit or the context makes it clearly past; however the rule may over-match some past-tense cases—prioritize precision by excluding when 也/不 follow 再.
