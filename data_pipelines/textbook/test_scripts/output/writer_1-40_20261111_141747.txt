**Point:** Expressing change of state, "now," "anymore," or "already" with sentence-final 了

**Covers:** Sentences where a clause-final 了 marks a new situation or change. The rule covers these cases:
- A pronoun or subject plus an adjective plus 了: 我饿了, 你胖了.
- Negation plus 了, meaning "not anymore": 我不去看电影了, 我已经不住这里了, 你不喜欢我了吗, 没有纸了, 手机没电了.
- Age with 了: 我20岁了.
- 当 + role + 了: 他当经理了.
- Fixed "now" events: 吃饭了, 开会了, 上课了, 下雨了, 到你了.
- 找到 + modifier + noun + 了: 找到新工作了.

**Does not cover:**
- 了 after a verb but before an object or complement (completion): 做完了工作, 买到了票, 我买了, 他们到了
- Bare completed-verb sentences: 我找到了, 我们都去了
