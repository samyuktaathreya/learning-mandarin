## 3-80
**Point:** Expressing indifference or concession with "X就X(点儿)吧" — repeating a word/phrase with 就 and ending with 吧 to show resignation or "I don't care" attitude.

**Covers:** 
- Verb + 就 + Verb + 吧 (e.g., 走就走吧)
- Adjective + 就 + Adjective + 吧 (e.g., 慢就慢吧)
- Negation + Verb/Adj. + 就 + Negation + Verb/Adj. + 吧 (e.g., 不帮就不帮吧)
- Optional 点儿 between repeated element and 吧 (e.g., 忙就忙点儿吧)

**Does not cover:** 
- Simple suggestions with 吧 without the X就X repetition (我们走吧)
- 就 in conditional structures (只要...就, 如果...就)
- 就 for other meanings (emphasis, earliness, scope, indifference without the repetition pattern)
- 吧 for asking opinions or softening statements without 就 and repetition
- Constructions where 就 and 吧 are present but not forming the X就X吧 pattern

```rule
match: _ 就/AD _ 吧/SP $
unless: 只要|如果|既然|要是|假|万一|再不|一旦|就算|就是|只有|一下|等|说着|动着 ... 就
unless: ^ ... 就/AD ... 就/AD
```

**Notes:** The core pattern requires (1) a word or short phrase, (2) 就 tagged as AD, (3) repetition of that same word/phrase (may include optional 点儿/CD or particles), (4) 吧/SP at clause end. The word/phrase can be a single verb, adjective, or negation+verb/adj. combination. The second "unless" prevents matching when 就 appears earlier in the clause in a different pattern (like conditionals or other uses).
