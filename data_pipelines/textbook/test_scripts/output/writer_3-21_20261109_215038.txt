## 3-21
**Point:** Using 自从/从/自 to mark the starting point of a past action or event, often with 以来/以后/后 to show how things have changed since then.
**Covers:** Sentences with 自从, 从, or 自 followed by a time/event phrase, typically followed by 以来, 以后, or 后, establishing a past starting point with consequences in the main clause.
**Does not cover:** Uses of 从 to mark spatial starting points or future starting points; uses of these words in other contexts not expressing a past temporal starting point.

```rule
match: /P ... /LC
match: 自从/P
match: 从/P
match: 自/P
unless: 从 ... /LC?$ ... 从
unless: 从/P ... /LC? ... 从/P
```

**Notes:** The rule looks for prepositions (自从, 从, or 自) in a clause that is followed by a localizer (以来, 以后, 后, etc.). The main structural clue is the pairing of the preposition with a localizer ending. The unless clauses guard against coordinate structures where multiple 从/自 phrases appear, which might indicate a different grammatical point. The tokenization splits clauses at punctuation, so the starting point clause and the result clause are separate.
