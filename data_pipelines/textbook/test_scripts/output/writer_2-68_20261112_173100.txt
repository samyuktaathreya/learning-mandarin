## 2-68
**Point:** Cause and effect relationship expressed with 因为 (because) and/or 所以 (so/therefore) across clause boundaries

**Covers:** 
- Result clause followed by 因为 + reason clause
- Cause clause followed by 所以 + result clause  
- 因为 + cause clause followed by 所以 + result clause

**Does not cover:**
- Sentences using 因为...而... (because...as a result...) structure, which uses 而 to show effect within a single clause
- Other causal connectors like 由于

```rule
match: ... 因为/P ... ‖ ...
match: ... ‖ 所以/AD ...
match: ^ 因为/P ... ‖ ... 所以/AD ...
unless: 而/MSP
```

**Notes:** The three match lines capture the three structures: (1) result then reason, (2) reason then result, (3) full 因为...所以... pattern. The unless line excludes the 因为...而... structure used in counter-examples, which is a different grammar point. Sentences may have 因为 or 所以 alone (not both), which is acceptable per the official examples and grammar explanation.
