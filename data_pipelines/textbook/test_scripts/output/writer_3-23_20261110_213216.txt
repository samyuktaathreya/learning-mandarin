**Point:** Using 为 as a preposition to express "for" (beneficiary or purpose)
**Covers:** (1) 为 + beneficiary/goal + verb/adjective showing an action or emotion done for them (including 是…为…的 and 不为…); (2) the 为(了)…而… structure expressing purpose with effort or action
**Does not cover:** 给 + person + verb ("give/for"), which belongs to easier points; bare 为了 clauses without 而 (3-26); 因为 (because); 以为 (mistakenly think); 为 as main verb or copula (是/变为/成为); fixed expressions such as 以…为, 作为, 为期, 为此, 为什么, 为难, 为的是, 为…所…

```rule
match: 为/P _ ... /VV|VA
match: 为了/P ... 而 ... /VV|VA
unless: 以 ... 为
unless: 为/VC
unless: 为 什么
unless: 为 的 是
unless: 为 ... 所
unless: 作为
unless: 因为
unless: 以为
```

**Notes:** 为 is matched only when tagged as a preposition (P) and followed by a word and then a verb or adjective in the same clause. This covers 为我们高兴, 为孩子而活, 是为你准备的 and 为公司的发展贡献. Plain 为了 is still excluded, but 为了…而… with a verb after 而 is matched, as in 他为了能去日本而苦学日语. The 给 line was removed: 给 + person + verb (给我打电话, 给她办派对) is taught in much easier points and was wrongly matching their sentences. The unless lines block 以…为, 为什么, 为的是, 为…所 passives, 作为, 因为 and 以为.
