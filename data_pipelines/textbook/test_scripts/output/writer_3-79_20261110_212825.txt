## 3-79
**Point:** Expressing "already" with 都⋯⋯了, emphasizing that something has already happened with a tone implying the speaker's attitude
**Covers:** Sentences with 都 followed by a predicate (verb or adjective) and 了, where 都 appears at or near the start of a clause and emphasizes completion/state change
**Does not cover:** Uses of 都 meaning "all/everyone" (with quantified subjects or question words), 都 with 已经, 都 with 从来, 都 in 不管/无论/何况/连 constructions, 都 with 全, 都 with 除了, or 都 meaning "no matter what/when"

```rule
match: ^ 都/AD ... /AS|SP|VA|VV ... 了/AS|SP
unless: ^ 都/AD ... /DT ... 都/AD
unless: ^ 都/AD ... /CS
unless: ^ 都/AD ... 从来/AD
unless: ^ 都/AD ... 不管/CS
unless: ^ 都/AD ... 无论/CS
unless: ^ 都/AD ... 连/AD
unless: ^ 都/AD ... 全|全都
unless: ^ 除了/P
unless: _ 什么 ... 都/AD
unless: _ 谁 ... 都/AD
unless: _ 哪|哪儿|哪里 ... 都/AD
unless: _ 什么时候 ... 都/AD
unless: _ 怎么 ... 都/AD
unless: _ 多少 ... 都/AD
unless: ^ ... 也/AD 都/AD
unless: 何况/AD
unless: 更不用说
```

**Notes:** The pattern requires 都 at or near the clause start (^ 都) followed eventually by a completed action or state (marked by 了), with a verb or adjective in between. The unless clauses exclude all the confounding uses from the HSK grammar list: quantifier+都, question words+都, 从来都, and all the special constructions (不管, 无论, 连, etc.). The rule looks for AS (aspect particle like 过), SP (sentence-final 了), or VA/VV tags as predicates to catch both verb and adjective forms. The pattern avoids matching when a determiner precedes 都 (ruling out "每天都" type patterns) or when there's an intervening CS (subordinating conjunction).
