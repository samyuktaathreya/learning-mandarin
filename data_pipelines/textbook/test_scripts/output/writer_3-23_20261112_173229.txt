## 3-23
<!-- written by claude-sonnet-5-5 on 2026-11-10; edit freely -->
<!-- revised by claude on 2026-11-09; edit freely -->
**Point:** Using 为 (and, for the "for someone" sense, 给) as a preposition to express "for" (beneficiary or purpose)
**Covers:** (1) 为 + beneficiary/goal + verb/adjective showing an action or emotion done for them (including 是…为…的 and 不为…); (2) the 为(了)…而… structure expressing purpose with effort or action; (3) 给 + person + verb, as in the wiki's 给/为 comparison (请给我拿一双筷子, 我给你打电话, 给他道歉)
**Does not cover:** bare 为了 clauses without 而 (3-26); 因为 (because); 以为 (mistakenly think); 为 as main verb or copula (是/变为/成为); fixed expressions such as 以…为, 作为, 为期, 为此, 为什么, 为难, 为的是, 为…所…; 给 as a main verb (给我一杯水)

```rule
match: 为/P _ ... /VV|VA
match: 为了/P ... 而 ... /VV|VA
match: 给/P _ /AD? /VV
unless: 以 ... 为
unless: 为/VC
unless: 为 什么
unless: 为 的 是
unless: 为 ... 所
unless: 作为
unless: 因为
unless: 以为
```

**Notes:** 为 is matched only when tagged as a preposition (P) and followed by a word and then a verb or adjective in the same clause. This covers 为我们高兴, 为孩子而活, 是为你准备的 and 为公司的发展贡献. Plain 为了 is still excluded, but 为了…而… with a verb after 而 is matched, as in 他为了能去日本而苦学日语. The 给 line is restored: the labeled examples from the 给 vs. 为 comparison (请给我拿一双筷子, 我给你打电话, 你别忘了给我回邮件, 同事给我介绍了…, 给他道歉, 老板给我提了…, 上海给我留下了…) are 3-23. It is kept tight, requiring 给 tagged P, then one word (the person), an optional adverb, and a verb, so 给 as a main verb is not matched. The unless lines block 以…为, 为什么, 为的是, 为…所 passives, 作为, 因为 and 以为.
