## 3-69
**Point:** Expressing "if… then…" with 要是/要不是/要
**Covers:** Conditional sentences with 要是, 要不是, or 要 (in specific patterns) followed by a consequence clause, often marked by 就
**Does not cover:** Other uses of 就 (emphasis, sequence, temporal, "then" without conditional); 就是 constructions (even if, emphasis, identity); other conditional structures like 如果, 只要, 假如, 万一, 一旦

```rule
match: 要是/CS ... 就/AD
match: 要不是/AD ... 就/AD
match: 要不是/CS ... 就/AD
match: 要/CS ... 就/AD
match: _ 要是/CS ... 就/AD
match: _ 要不/AD ... 就/AD
match: 要不是/AD ... $
unless: 就/AD 是/VC
unless: 就是/AD
unless: 就/AD 要/VV
```

**Notes:** The rule prioritizes precision by requiring both a conditional marker (要是, 要不是, or 要) and typically a 就 in the consequence clause. The first four patterns handle the standard structure. Patterns 5–6 capture subject-before-marker word order. Pattern 7 (要不是 without 就) covers "if it weren't for" constructions which end the sentence. The unless clauses exclude 就是 emphasis/identity constructions and 就要 temporal constructions. The tagger sometimes tags 要不是 as AD instead of CS; both are included since they appear in labeled examples.
