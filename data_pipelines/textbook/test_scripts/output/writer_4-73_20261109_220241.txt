## 4-73
**Point:** Using 还 + predicate/noun phrase (+ 呢/吗) to express surprise, sarcasm, or astonishment about an irrational or unexpected situation

**Covers:** 
- 还 + verb/adjective phrase + 呢 (expressing irony: "He studied abroad, yet his English is terrible!")
- 还 + noun phrase + 呢 (expressing sarcasm: "What a 'good friend'!")
- 还 + verb phrase + 吗 (rhetorical question: "Would you really believe this kind of person?")
- Sentences where 还 signals that the subject contradicts expectations (e.g., "This kid is brilliant!" with 还真)

**Does not cover:**
- 还是 (different grammatical structure: choices, "had better", contrast)
- 还 + 好/可以/不错 (moderating adjectives)
- 还有 (expressing "in addition" or "there is/remains")
- 还在/还不/还没 (expressing continuation or negation of completion)
- 还 in comparisons (比...还)
- 还 meaning "and also" (coordinating two parallel actions)
- 不仅...还 (not-only-but-also structure)
- 还好...不然 (expressing luck/fortunate circumstance)

```rule
match: 还/AD ... (/SP|吗)? $
match: 还/AD /NN.* 呢/SP
unless: 还是/AD
unless: 还/AD 好/VA
unless: 还/AD 有/VE
unless: 还/AD 在/AD
unless: 还/AD 在/VV
unless: 还/AD 不
unless: 还/AD 没/AD
unless: 还/AD 没有/VE
unless: /P 还/AD
unless: 比/P ... 还/AD
unless: 除了/P
unless: 不仅|不但|不只/AD
unless: 不然|但是|可是/CC
unless: 还/AD 款/NN
```

**Notes:** The rule targets the core pattern of 还 at the start of a clause followed by a predicate/noun, often ending with 呢 or 吗. It excludes 还是 entirely (different word), 还有 (different meaning), and 还 in continuation patterns (还在/还不/还没). The pattern `... (/SP|�mas)? $` captures both statement and rhetorical question forms. Noun phrases like "名牌儿呢" are matched by the second pattern. Counter-examples like "还好我们买了..." are excluded by checking for 还好 patterns and preceding context words like 除了, 不仅, etc.
