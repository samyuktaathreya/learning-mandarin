## 2-25

**Point:** Using 对 as a preposition meaning "to/towards/regarding" (the target of an action or emotion), or as a verb meaning "to face/point at/match" (对着, 对上, 对不上). Also the contrasting 跟 uses taught alongside it: 跟 as a preposition or conjunction ("with/and/same as"), and as a verb (跟着, 跟不上).

**Covers:** 对 + Object + Verb/Adjective (对我笑, 对她说, 对工作很认真, 对你不满意); 对着/对上/对不上; 对于 used as a preposition; 跟 + person + 说; 跟…一起; 跟…一样; 跟着; 跟不上.

**Does not cover:** 对...来说 (HSK 3-40); 对...而言; 对方 (the pronoun "other party"); tag questions 对不对/对吗; 对 as adjective meaning "correct".

```rule
match: 对/P
match: 对于/P
match: 对/VA 于/P
match: 对/VA|VV 着/AS
match: 对/VA|VV 上/VV
match: 对/VV 不/AD 上/VV
match: 跟/P|CC|VV
unless: 而言
unless: 来说
unless: 来 说
unless: 对方
unless: 对 不 对
unless: 对 吗
```

**Notes:** Prepositional 对 is tagged 对/P and matches on its own, as does 对于/P, which the labelled examples treat as part of this point. The 对/VA 于/P line covers a tokenizer split inside one clause. The verbal 对着, 对上 and 对不上 are matched explicitly because the tagger gives 对 either VA or VV. 跟 is matched whether tagged P (跟…说, 跟…一起), CC (跟…一样) or VV (跟着, 跟不上), because the labelled examples cover all of these. The unless lines still exclude 来说, 而言, 对方 and the tag questions 对不对 and 对吗. The split form 对(于)…, where 对/VA is separated from 于/P by the bracket punctuation, is not matched. Bare "对，" as an answer is far too common to match safely.
