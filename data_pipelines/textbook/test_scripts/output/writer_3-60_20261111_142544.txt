## 3-60
**Point:** The adverb 也 (yě) meaning "also" or "too", used in a parallel "A…, also B…" structure. The earlier clause states something about one topic. The later clause has a different fronted topic, then the subject, then 也 before the verb or adjective. Example: 篮球他喜欢，排球他也喜欢。

**Covers:**
- Topic (noun) + Subject (pronoun) + 也 + Verb, in a clause parallel to an earlier one (面条儿我也爱吃。)
- The same with an adjective (这个我也很喜欢 type, with 也 before the adjective)
- The same with a negation after 也 (米饭我也不爱吃。)
- Simple parallel structures showing an "also" relationship between two similar clauses

**Does not cover:**
- Plain single-subject 也 sentences (我也喜欢。, 我也是学生。), which are easier points (1-03, 1-13)
- Conditional/concessive structures (即使...也, 就算...也, 就是...也, 哪怕...也, etc.) where 也 means "still/even so"
- Conditional sentences with 如果, 假如, 假使, 要是, 要不, 要不是 and similar
- Any sentence containing a subordinating conjunction (CS) such as 因为 or 虽然
- "Never again" with 再也不/没
- 甚至连...也
- 宁可...也
- 一点儿也不 and 一 + Measure Word + Noun + 也 + 不/没
- 什么/谁 + Noun + 也
- 连...也
- 倒也
- Structures with preceding markers like 别说, 就是, 就算, 不但, 无论, 不管, etc.

```rule

match: ^ /NN|NR /PN 也/AD 不/AD /VV|VA
unless: 再/AD ... 也/AD
unless: 甚至
```

**Notes:** The rule requires a fronted noun topic at the start of the clause, directly followed by a pronoun subject, then 也 and a verb or adjective. This is the topic-comment form in the official examples (排球他也喜欢, 米饭我也爱吃). It therefore does not fire on the basic "Subject + 也 + Verb" sentences (我也喜欢, 他们也会去吗), which belong to easier points. The `unless:` lines remove the concessive, emphatic, conditional and "never again" structures, including 别说, 再也, 连 and 甚至. They also remove any sentence containing a subordinating conjunction.
