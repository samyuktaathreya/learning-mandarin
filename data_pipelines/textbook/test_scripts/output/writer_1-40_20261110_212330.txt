## 1-40
**Point:** Expressing change of state, "now," "anymore," or "already" with sentence-final 了

**Covers:** Sentences where 了 at the end of a clause indicates a new situation or change (adjectives becoming true, verbs describing new states, negated verbs/nouns indicating something no longer exists or is no longer done), including: "I'm hungry" (state change), "It's raining" (new situation), "I don't want to eat anymore" (negation + change), "He's coming now" (new situation), "There's no paper anymore" (negated noun + change).

**Does not cover:** 
- 了 after a verb but before an object or complement (completion/perfective aspect): 做完了工作, 买到了票
- 了 in questions with 吗: 你吃饭了吗？, 他来了吗？
- Expressions with 太...了 (excessively): 太脏了, 太好了
- Expressions with ...点儿: 短了点儿, 咸了点儿
- Expressions with ...死了 (intensifiers): 饿死了, 累坏了
- Expressions with 快...了 (about to happen): 快下雨了, 快到了
- Duration expressions with double 了: 睡了一天了, 开了两个小时了
- Result complements: 完了, 坏了 in 说完了, 高兴坏了
- 过...了 (experiential aspect): 吃过了, 洗过澡了
- Other advanced le constructions and complement forms


**Notes:** The core pattern is sentence-final 了 (tagged SP or AS) that indicates a change of state. This excludes 了 that comes before objects or complements (tagged AS mid-clause). The rule requires 了 at the end of a clause ($). Negation patterns with 不 or 没有/VE are included. Watch for false positives with 吗 (questions should be excluded), 太 (excessively), and result complements like 坏/死. Duration double-了 is excluded via the "one/CD in middle" pattern.
