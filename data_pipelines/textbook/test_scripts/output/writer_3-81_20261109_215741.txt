## 3-81
**Point:** Rhetorical challenge with 什么 after verbs or adjectives
**Covers:** Verb/Adjective + 什么 (+ repetition) (+ 啊/呀/吧) used to form a rhetorical question expressing disapproval or incredulity
**Does not cover:** 什么 as a genuine question word; 什么都/也 expressing "everything"; 什么的 ending a list; 什么 in compounds like 什么时候

```rule
match: /VV 什么/PN /VV
match: /VA 什么/PN /VA?
match: /VV 什么/DT
match: /VA 什么/DT /VA
unless: 都/AD
unless: 也/AD
unless: 什么/DT 的/DEG
unless: 什么/PN 都/AD
unless: 什么/PN 也/AD
```

**Notes:** 
- The pattern captures verb-什么-verb (你笑什么笑) and adjective-什么-adjective (胖什么胖啊) as the clearest rhetorical challenge patterns.
- Also matches verb-什么 (玩儿什么) and adjective-什么 (舒服什么啊) where the verb/adjective is not immediately repeated but context is rhetorical.
- Uses /DT to capture cases where 什么 functions as determiner (开什么玩笑, 还吃什么早饭) before a noun object, still forming a rhetorical challenge.
- The unless clauses filter out "什么都/也" (expressing "everything") and "什么的" (list-ending).
- Particle 啊/SP, 呀/IJ, 吧/SP are optional and don't need explicit matching (appear after the pattern).
- Pattern searches within one clause only, respecting punctuation boundaries.
