## 2-17
<!-- written by claude-sonnet-5-5 on 2026-11-10; edit freely -->
**Point:** Expressing "then" with the adverb 就 (jiù) in conditional and causal structures

**Covers:** Sentences whose first clause sets up a condition or cause (如果, 要是, 要不是, 假如, 假使, 既然, 只要, 的话, or 要我说), and whose second clause gives the consequence, result or suggestion, usually led by 就. This includes "if...then", "since...then", "as long as...then", "if it weren't for...", and 的话...就 sentences. It also covers the short, simple form where the setup is implied or sits in the previous clause: 就 opening a result or suggestion clause (就去看电影吧, 就在这家店吃饭吧, 就不要喝酒了), 我们就…吧/了, 他就哭了, 喜欢就拿走吧, and 有事/有空 + request.

**Does not cover:** 就 for earliness or timing (他早就知道了), scope (就这个问题), small quantity (就他知道, 我就去过一次), near-future 就要...了, "even if" patterns (就算/就是...也), emphasis with 就是, "as you like" and verb-repetition patterns (想吃就吃, 要买就买), 不是...就是, 一...就, and verbs repeated with 着 (说着说着就...).

```rule
match: 如果|既然|只要|假如|假使|要是/CS
match: ^ 要不是/AD|CS
match: ^ 要/CS|VV 我 说 $
match: 的话/SP 就/AD /VV|P|AD
match: ^ 就/AD /VV|P
match: ^ 就/AD 不要|别|多|先/AD
match: 我们|咱们 就/AD 不/AD? /VV ... 吧|了 $
match: 我们|咱们 就/AD 不|一起/AD
match: /PN 就/AD /VV 了/AS|SP $
match: ^ 喜欢 就/AD /VV ... 吧/SP $
match: ^ 有/VE 事|空|问题|困难/NN 给|来|找|问|打
unless: 就是
unless: 就算
unless: 就/AD 算/VV
unless: 就/AD 是/VC
unless: 不是 ... 就是
unless: 就/AD 要/VV ... 了/SP|AS $
unless: 一/AD ... 就/AD
unless: 着/AS 就/AD
unless: 才/AD
unless: 就/AD 在/VV
```

**Notes:** Patterns match within a single clause, so they cannot link a first clause to a second. The broad "pronoun + 就 + verb" lines stay removed, because they matched scope, quantity and emphasis uses (我就去过一次, 我就不告诉你). Instead, the short result or suggestion forms are matched narrowly. A clause that starts with 就 + verb or preposition (就去…, 就在…, 就给你…) matches, as does 就 + 不要/别/多/先. 我们/咱们 + 就 + verb must end in 吧 or 了, or be followed by 不 or 一起. Any pronoun + 就 + verb + 了 at clause end is also matched (他就哭了). 喜欢就…吧 and clause-initial 有事/有空 + request (有事给我打电话) are matched as set phrases. The unless lines still exclude the non-target uses of 就: 就是, 就算, 就要…了, 不是…就是, 一…就, 着 repetition, 才 contrasts and verbal 在. One known miss is that 要我说，他就是… is blocked by the 就是 exclusion.
