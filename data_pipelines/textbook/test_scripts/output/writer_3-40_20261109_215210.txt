## 3-40
**Point:** 对...来说 and other phrases with 来说 to express "for/to someone" or set perspective
**Covers:** Sentences with the pattern (对 + [person/group] + 来说) or ([adverb] + 来说) that introduce a viewpoint or scope before a predicate
**Does not cover:** Sentences using 而言 instead of 来说; sentences where 就 is the main preposition; other uses of 对 as a preposition meaning "towards/to/against"; sentences where 来说 appears but isn't paired with 对 or an adverb for perspective-setting

```rule
match: 来/MSP 说/VV
unless: 而言/LC
```

**Notes:** The key distinguishing feature is that 来/MSP 说/VV must appear as a unit (never 来说/VV as one token, since the tagger splits it). The most reliable way to exclude 对...而言 is to check that 而言/LC does not appear in the sentence. The pattern is very simple because both 对 + person + 来说 and adverb + 来说 constructions both rely on the same 来/MSP 说/VV sequence. The unless clause prevents matching sentences that use the synonymous but different 而言/LC construction.
