## 3-48
**Point:** Potential complements showing whether an action is possible: Verb + 得/不 + complement (result, adjective, or direction), plus Verb + 得了/不了, 得下/不下, 得起/不起, 得动/不动.

**Covers:**
- Verb + 不 + complement (cannot do): 听不懂, 看不见, 洗不干净, 去不了, 吃不下, 买不起, 爬不上去
- Verb + 得 + a fixed result or direction complement (can do): 听得懂, 看得见, 吃得完, 帮得了, 放得下, 走得动, 爬得上去
- The complement must come from a closed list of result, direction and 了/下/起/动 complements.

**Does not cover:**
- 不得不 "have to/must"
- 差不多 "about the same"
- 少不了 / 免不了 "unavoidable"
- Degree and state complements with 得 (画得很好, 说得清楚, 做得好, 堵得要命, 得不得了, 得很)
- 害得 "causing an undesirable result"
- 对不起 / 了不起 as fixed expressions
- 觉得/认为/感觉 + 不 + adjective (an opinion, not a potential complement)
- A-not-A questions (懂不懂, 到不到)
- 得了 as a standalone "okay/en
