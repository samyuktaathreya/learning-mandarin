## 2-32
**Point:** The aspect particle 过 after a verb says the subject has (or has never) had an experience, as in 去过中国 and 没学过这个词.

**Covers:** A verb followed by 过 as an aspect particle, in affirmative, negative (没), or question (吗) sentences, with or without an object. Examples: 我也吃过日本菜, 我们都没坐过飞机, 你学过中文吗. The tagger labels the particle either AS or ASP, so both are accepted. It also covers 见过, which the tagger sometimes tags 见/VV 过/VV.

**Does not cover:**
- 经过, 通过, 不过, 莫过于, 超过, 过于, 过年, 过去 and 过来. These are single words or other structures, so a bare 过 never matches inside them.
- Sentences with a 不过 ("but") clause, such as 这家餐厅我没去过，不过我听说还不错. These are classed under the 不过 point, so they are excluded.
- Direction-complement uses such as 走过来 and 搬过去.
- Motion-past uses such as 走过这条街, which describe passing through a place, not an experience. Verbs of this kind (走, 跑, 飞, 游, 跳) are excluded.
- Completion uses where 过 is followed by 了, such as 吃过饭了. These are borderline, so they are excluded.

```rule
match: /VV 过/AS
match: /VV 过/ASP
match: 见 过/VV
unless: 走|跑|飞|游|跳 过
unless: 过/AS ... 了
unless: 过/ASP ... 了
unless: 过/AS 了
unless: 过/ASP 了
unless: 不过
```

**Notes:**
- The main patterns need a VV directly before a 过 tagged AS or ASP. The tagger uses both labels for the particle, so 我也吃过日本菜 (tagged ASP) was previously missed. The only VV-tagged 过 allowed is the one after 见, because the tagger gets that example wrong. Allowing 过/VV after other verbs would let in 走过来 and 过上 (as in 过上好日子).
- The unless lines are deliberately conservative to favor precision. Some real experience sentences, such as 我走过这条路, are lost.
- Unless lines are checked over the whole clause, so any 了 after 过 in the same clause blocks the match. The 不过 line is meant to block the whole sentence, so a 不过 clause after a 没去过 clause also blocks it.
