## 1-20
**Point:** Structural particles 的, 地, 得 for modifying nouns and verbs

**Covers:** 
- 的 (de) for possessive/modifying nouns: Noun/Pronoun + 的 + Noun; Adj + 的 + Noun; Phrase + 的 + Noun
- 地 (de) for adverbial phrases: Adj + 地 + Verb
- Reduplication patterns with adjectives: AA的, AABB的, AABB地+Verb
- Standalone 的 at clause end when it clearly follows one of the above patterns

**Does not cover:**
- Modal particle 的 in statements (他会好的)
- 有的 as "some" (有的人, 有的是)
- 的话 as conditional/topic marker
- 似的 as "like/similar to"
- 什么的 as list-ending marker
- 之类的 as "such as/et cetera"
- 得 as degree complement after verbs (做得很好 when just showing result quality, not reduplicated adj modifying)
- 是...的 emphasis/purpose constructions (我是来玩的)
- Determiner uses (这的, 那的)

```rule
match: /DEG|DEC|DEV ... /NN|VV
match: /VA /DEV /VV
match: /VA /DEC /NN
match: /VA /DEG /NN
match: /VA /VA /DEG? /NN?
match: /VA /VA /VA /VA /DEV|DEC|DEG /NN?|VV
match: /DT? /NN /DEG /NN
match: _ /VV /DEC /NN
match: _ /VV /DEG /NN
match: /PN /DEG /NN
match: /VA /DEC $
match: /VA /DEV $
match: /PN /VV /AS|DEC /NN
unless: 的话/SP
unless: 有的/PN
unless: 之/DEG 类/M 的/DEG
unless: 什么/PN 的/DEG
unless: 似的/AD
unless: 是/VC ... 的/SP
unless: /PN 是/VC ... 的/SP
unless: /VV 得/DER ... /VA $ 
```

**Notes:** The rule prioritizes precision by matching clear structural uses: possessive/attributive 的 (DEG/DEC) with nouns, adverbial 地 (DEV) with verbs, and adjective-based modification patterns. Reduplicated adjectives are matched as sequences of VA tags. The pattern `_ /VV /DEC|/DEG /NN` captures relative clauses. End-of-clause patterns catch standalone 的 after clear attributive structures. Unless clauses exclude common confusable grammar points that use identical character forms but serve different functions. Note that 得 as degree complement (verb + 得 + adjective/adverb) is generally not included as it's HSK 1-31 (degree complement), though the rule focuses on the structural particle uses listed for this point.
