## 2-16
**Point:** Frequency and repetition adverbs: 重新, 经常, 老, 老是, 又

**Covers:** 
- 老是/老 expressing habitual or repeated action with a frustrated tone
- 经常/常常 expressing frequent action
- 又 expressing an action happening again (past or imminent)
- 重新 expressing doing something again from the start

**Does not cover:**
- 又 used for emphasis of negation (又不/又没 meaning "definitely not")
- 又 in repeated action patterns with 了...又... structure
- 又 expressing both A and B qualities (又...又... with adjectives)
- 既...又... patterns
- 老 as an adjective meaning "old" (老师, 老人, 老家)
- 常 as an adjective meaning "frequent/regular"

```rule
match: 老是/AD ... /VV|VA
match: 老/AD ... /VV|VA
match: 经常/AD ... /VV
match: 常常/AD ... /VV
match: 常/AD /VV
match: 又/AD /VV|VA
match: 又/AD 不/AD /VV|VA
match: 又/AD 没/AD /VV
match: 又/AD /VV ... 了/SP
match: 又/AD 要/VV
match: 又/AD 能/VV
match: 又/AD 可以/VV
match: 重新/AD ... /VV
unless: ^ 既/AD
unless: /VV 了/AS 又/AD /VV
unless: /VV 了/AS 又/CC
unless: 又/AD /VA 又/AD /VA
unless: 又/CC
unless: 又/AD 不/AD /VV ‖ /VV
unless: 又/AD 没/AD /VV ‖ /VV
unless: /VA ‖ 又/AD 不/AD
unless: /VA ‖ 又/AD 没/AD
unless: 经常/VA 的/DEC
```

**Notes:** 
- The pattern for 老/老是 requires a verb or adjective after to distinguish from the noun adjective uses (老师, 老人). Often there are adverbs between (不, 很, 这么) but these aren't required.
- For 又 expressing "again" in past: look for 又 + verb + 了, or 又 + 不/没 + verb pattern. Exclude cases where 又 is preceded by a perfective 了 (which signals repeated actions). 
- For 又 expressing imminent repetition: 又 + 要/能/可以 + verb, often followed by 了.
- Exclude the "又A又B" adjective patterns (both tagged VA or CC variant).
- Exclude negation emphasis (又不/又没 as standalone complaint without expressing repetition).
- The 经常/VA 的 pattern (expressing frequency as a property) counts as the grammar point.
- 常 by itself (single character) counts when followed by verb, used in colloquial speech for future frequency.
