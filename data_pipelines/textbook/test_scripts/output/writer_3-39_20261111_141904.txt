## 3-39
**Point:** 从⋯⋯起 / 离⋯⋯ — expressing a starting point in time or a distance/duration
**Covers:** 
- 从 + time/event + 起/以后 (starting from a time or event)
- 离 + event/time + 还/还有 + duration (distance until a future event)
- 离 + past event + duration (time elapsed since a past event)
- 从 + place/person + (other content) expressing origin/source of action

**Does not cover:**
- 起来 as a direction complement or resultative (站起来, 吵起来, 听起来, 看起来 = appearance)
- 从 as adverb meaning "always/never" (从不, 从来)
- 从 in the phrase 从事 (to engage in a profession)
- 一起 meaning "together"
- 比起 (compared to)
- 从 + place + 来/去 as simple motion from origin

```rule
match: 从/P _ (起/LC|以后/LC) | 离/P _ (还|还有)/AD (有/VE)?
unless: 起来/VV | 从不/AD | 从来/AD | 从事/VV | 一起/AD | 比/VV 起/VV
```

**Notes:** The rule captures the two main patterns: (1) 从 + temporal/event element + 起 or 以后, and (2) 离 + element + 还/还有 + (有). The unless clauses filter out direction complements (起来), adverbial uses (从不/从来), the verb 从事, the adverb 一起, and the comparative 比起. The pattern allows flexible content between the preposition and the endpoint marker to accommodate different sentence structures.
