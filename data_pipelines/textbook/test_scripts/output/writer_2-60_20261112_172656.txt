## 2-60
**Point:** The "是⋯⋯的" construction for emphasizing details of past events (time, location, manner, agent)
**Covers:** Sentences with 是 + [emphasized information] + verb + 的/了, where 的 is a modal particle or aspect particle marking emphasis on details like when, where, how, or who did something
**Does not cover:** Possession (的 as structural particle), adjective modification (的), "有的是" (meaning "plenty of"), "的话" (topic marker), reduplication patterns, concession patterns (Adj是Adj就是), purpose patterns (来/去/用来), or simple identity statements (是 without emphasis)

```rule
match: VC ... /VV ... /AS|SP
unless: /VC (/AD|CS)* /VC (/AD)* (/NN|VA)
unless: /VC (/AD)* /VC
unless: /VC /NN
unless: /VC /P .*
unless: PN /PN 是/VC
unless: 有的/PN 是/VC
```

**Notes:** 
- The core pattern requires 是 (VC tag), followed by content, followed by a verb (VV), followed by 的/了 (AS or SP tag)
- First unless blocks concession ("Adj是Adj") and rhetorical patterns ("不是不是")
- Second unless blocks simple identity statements (是 + noun)
- Third unless blocks purpose/ability patterns (是 + preposition)
- Fourth unless blocks pronominal antecedents that aren't relevant
- Fifth unless blocks "有的是" (abundance construction)
- The rule prioritizes recall for the classic 是⋯⋯的 pattern while excluding common false positives from counter-examples
